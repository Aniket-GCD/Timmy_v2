How the workflow runs

Capture → Review → Approve → Export

Human review before export to QuickBooks is the rule, not an optional setting. The loop below is the only path an entry can take.

1CaptureStart, switch, end, or add missing time as drafts.
2ReviewEntries stay editable and visible end-of-day.
3ApproveApprove draft entries one by one; the rest stay open.
4ExportOnly approved time becomes a QuickBooks-ready CSV.

Privacy boundary · v1 guardrails

What this prototype will not do

The goal is trustworthy capture, not monitoring. These boundaries hold for v1.

No surveillanceNo screen recording or keystroke capture. Every change is an explicit, logged action you can audit.
Drafts, not recordsEntries are editable drafts until you approve them — never silent final billing.
You stay the authorityApproval is required before anything is exported. The accountant decides.
No QuickBooks writebackv1 stops at a reviewed, QuickBooks-ready CSV. No direct writeback into your books.
